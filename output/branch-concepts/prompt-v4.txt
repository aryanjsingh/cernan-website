== STRUCTURE: RANDOM AND NATURAL ==

== THE MACHINE UNDERNEATH ==
The limb was built, not grown: aged, weathered dark gunmetal / graphite plating and segmented sleeves, with rust, patina and scratches. Where the moss is thinner you can see:
- machined ring joints and riveted collars at SOME of the forks (not all, and different sizes);
- a few round sensor/hub nodes at forks, half covered in moss, some cracked, a couple faintly glowing;
- inlaid channels / grooves running along the limbs, with a thin fiber-optic or glass core that still glows faintly pale cyan (#bff0ff) in places, disappearing under moss and reappearing further along;
- a few loose cables and thin conduits hanging down, tangled with vines.
The tech must stay subtle, worn and partly hidden. No neon, no clean surfaces, no sci-fi glow overload. The faint glowing channels must still be visible in several continuous stretches on the main limbs (a later SVG animation will send light pulses along them), so do not bury every channel.
